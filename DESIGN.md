# DESIGN DIRECTION: VORTEX PADEL ARENA

Reading this as: Padel booking & service platform for urban padel sports community, in a High-Performance Urban Arena visual style, dial ENERGY 2 / RHYTHM 2 / MOTION 2.

## Dials
- Dial: ENERGY 2 / RHYTHM 2 / MOTION 2
- Energy: 2 (Balanced athletic energy, high contrast, crisp sports-tech clarity)
- Rhythm: 2 (Structured sections with purposeful layout changes for booking, match finder, coach roster, pro shop)
- Motion: 2 (Tactile transitions on court slot selection, drawer transitions, interactive feedback)

## Identity & Brand
- Name: **VORTEX PADEL ARENA**
- Brand Voice: Energetic, focused, authentic to real padel sport culture, clear and functional.
- Motif: Padel court boundary lines, perforated racket surface accents, tactile time slot tiles.

## Color Palette
- Primary Dark Background: `#0B1120` (Deep Onyx / Court Slate)
- Surface / Card Dark: `#131E35`
- Border Dark: `#223254`
- Text Dark Theme: `#F8FAFC` (Headings), `#94A3B8` (Body/Meta)
- Primary Accent: `#D4E824` (Electric Volt / Court Lime - high visibility athletic accent)
- Secondary Accent: `#0284C7` (Sky Padel Blue - court surface indicator)
- Light Mode Base: `#F8FAFC`
- Light Mode Card: `#FFFFFF`
- Light Mode Border: `#E2E8F0`
- Light Mode Text: `#0F172A` (Headings), `#475569` (Body)
- Light Mode Accent: `#15803D` / `#0F172A` with `#84CC16` highlights for crisp contrast (WCAG AA compliant)

## Typography
- System Sans Stack: 'Plus Jakarta Sans', system-ui, -apple-system, sans-serif
- Hierarchy:
  - Display: 700 / 800 weight, tight tracking (-0.02em)
  - Subheadings: 600 weight, clear case
  - Body: 400 / 500 weight, 1.5 line height for optimal readability

## Design Rules & Intentionality (R-31)
- Why Volt Lime (`#D4E824`): Matches official padel ball vibrancy and provides immediate focus for active selections and primary action.
- Why Time Grid: Padel players book in 60/90-minute blocks; interactive slot matrix provides instant clarity on court availability without navigating multiple steps.
- Why Light/Dark Toggle: Players check slots outdoors in bright sunlight (needs crisp light mode) and book evening night-matches indoors (benefits from dark mode).
- Why Real Functionality: Every filter, slot toggle, coach booking, equipment selector, and drawer modal operates with responsive state and realistic feedback.
